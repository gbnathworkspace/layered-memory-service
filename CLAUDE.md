# Preferences

## How to give updates
- Keep it short: about 5–8 lines and readable in 30 seconds.
- Use 3 parts only: **Where I am → Results → Next 3 steps**.
- Lead with numbers (applied X, replies Y, interviews Z).
- At most 3 next steps, most important first.
- No long explanations or long lists of sources. Give details only when I ask ("more").
- Simple words; I write casually, so keep the reply casual too.
